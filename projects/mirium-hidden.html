01 CONCEPT

A PORTFOLIO THAT FEELS LIKE A JOURNEY.

MIRIUM.HIDD3N isn't meant to feel like a traditional portfolio.

Instead, the website is designed as a journey through the different layers of my creative mind.

As you move through the interface, each section reveals something different. Ideas. Experiments. Projects. Thoughts. Personality.

02 EXPERIENCE

UNCOVER ANOTHER LAYER.

The experience is intentionally inspired by interfaces found in games and futuristic operating systems.

Loading screens. System indicators. Experimental interfaces. Small interactions. Movement.

None of these elements exist purely for functionality. They help create an atmosphere.
